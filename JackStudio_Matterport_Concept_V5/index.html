INTERACTIVE 3D CONCEPT

Explore Jack Studio

Unlike the previous photo-switching demos, this is a walkable 3D space. Walls, display shelves, islands and products stay in place while you move around them.

  • Drag to turn your head 360°.
  • Click the floor to walk through the room.
  • WASD / ↑↓←→ to move and rotate, or use mobile arrows.
  • Walk around the store to take a guided 3D circuit, without playing any video.
  • Click white floor markers or the map to walk directly to a display.
  • View reference to see an actual photo of the corresponding shelf.

Concept only: layout, scale, unsupplied angles and product geometry are estimated. Your uploaded photos are incorporated as display textures; this is not a photogrammetry scan or Matterport export.
